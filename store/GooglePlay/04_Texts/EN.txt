Full description (max 4000):
Foldifyer turns a photo of your cat, dog or anyone in your family into a paper toy you can print at home.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Take a photo of your pet or a person.
2. Print the sheet on a home printer, on plain or thick paper.
3. Cut, fold and glue. The app draws the template itself, so every part fits.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Pets and people: cats, dogs, any animal, kids, parents, grandparents
• Outfits for every season: party hats, crowns, Santa hats and more
• Step-by-step building guide with a picture for every part
• Three difficulty levels and a colouring-page version for kids
• Your shelf: keep the whole family of figures in one place
• Print a set: the whole family together, with a house or a kennel for each
• Cards and gift boxes with your figure on them
• Share a picture of your figure with friends

FREE TO START, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Your first 2 figures are free.
Need more? Buy a pack once: 5 figures or 10 figures. Any pack also unlocks all outfits, face stickers, gift boxes, little houses, printable scenes and every new monthly collection — for good. No subscription, nothing renews, and figures never expire.

Your photo is used once to draw the figure and is not stored on our server. The printed faces are drawn by AI and are a likeness, not an exact copy.
