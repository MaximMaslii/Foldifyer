Delete your Foldifyer data

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Foldifyer has no accounts. Everything we store is linked to a random identifier on your phone, and you can delete it yourself in a few seconds.

Delete everything from inside the app

  • Open Foldifyer and go to the More tab.
  • Tap “Delete all my data”, then confirm.

This immediately and permanently removes from our server all your figures and their pictures.

Delete one figure

  • Open the figure → Delete this figure.

If you no longer have the app

Email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] and ask for deletion. If you still know your device identifier (shown in More), include it. Records of devices that have not been used for 24 months are deleted automatically.

What may be kept

A record of how many figures the device has used and bought (so the free figure cannot be refilled). Our AI provider may hold request data for a limited period (up to 55 days) for abuse monitoring before deleting it.
